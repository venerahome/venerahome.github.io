Where to buy property in Bulgaria: Burgas, Pomorie, Nessebar, Sveti Vlas or Sunny Beach?

Five places on the coast where I show properties every week. I compare price per square metre, winter life, rental demand and resale prospects.

Venera Khassanovareal estate consultant · Greece and Bulgaria
BulgariaVENI Where to buy property in Bulgaria: Burgas, Pomorie, Nessebar, Sveti Vlas or Sunny Beach?
In short
  • Burgas and Pomorie are for year-round living, Sunny Beach is for summer letting.
  • Nessebar and Sveti Vlas are a compromise: a resort in summer and quiet life in winter.
  • In resort complexes, always factor in the maintenance fee — it eats into your income.

The coast around Burgas is my everyday work. Clients often ask, “Where is best?” I answer with a question: “Why are you buying?” To live, to let or to holiday — everything depends on that. Below is a short portrait of each place, with real prices from my listings.

Burgas — a city for the whole year

A big city with an airport, hospitals, schools and jobs. Rentals are steady all year; according to Global Property Guide, the gross yield here is around 4.6%, one of the highest in the country.

Prices: about €1,700 per m² on average. Examples: a 62 m² one-bedroom in Lazur — €135,000; a 60 m² one-bedroom in a new build in Slaveykov — €100,000.

For whom: living, long-term letting, families with children. Why the city beats the resort on rentals, I explained separately.

Pomorie — a quiet town by the sea

A small town on a peninsula, with salt lakes, healing mud and a rhythm of its own. People live here in winter, not only holiday.

Example: a 74 m² one-bedroom — €147,340.

For whom: a calm life by the sea, retirees, remote workers.

Nessebar — the old town and the new districts

Old Nessebar is a UNESCO site, and the new town is growing towards Sunny Beach. Summer is busy, winter is quieter, but the town is alive.

Examples: 44 m² apartments in Kokalu — €65,000; a 73 m² one-bedroom — €108,000.

For whom: holidays and summer letting, with the option to live there in winter.

Sveti Vlas — sea and mountains

A resort at the foot of the Balkan Mountains, with a yacht marina and a more upmarket crowd. Prices are above the coastal average, but the air and the views are special.

Example: a 64 m² one-bedroom in Villa Venezia with no maintenance fee — €95,000.

For whom: holidays, seasonal letting at higher rates, and living for those who love winter quiet.

Sunny Beach — a resort for letting

The country's biggest resort. Studios and flats here are the most affordable: €1,200 to €1,800 per m². Summer demand is huge; in winter the resort is almost empty.

Examples: a 31 m² studio — €52,000; a 41 m² flat with a separate bedroom — €78,000.

For whom: summer letting and holidays. Not the best choice for living in winter.

Resale

Flats in Burgas and in good seaside complexes with fair fees are the easiest to resell. Cheap studios in older complexes sell more slowly — there are many buyers, but many listings too.

What matters everywhere

  • find out the maintenance fee and whether there are arrears;
  • check Act 16 and the complex's documents;
  • nightly letting requires registration under the Tourism Act.

My conclusion

  • To live — Burgas or Pomorie.
  • To let in summer — Sunny Beach or Nessebar.
  • For holidays and beauty — Sveti Vlas.
