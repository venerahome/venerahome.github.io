Residence in Bulgaria in 2026: every route in plain words

Does a flat by the sea give you the right to live in Bulgaria? Can a pensioner or a freelancer move here? I walk through the main grounds for long-term residence in Bulgaria — with figures now in euros.

In short
  • An ordinary flat purchase does not give residence — only a property investment of at least BGN 600,000, about €306,775.
  • The most accessible routes are a pension, the digital nomad visa, your own business, work, study or family.
  • First you get a national D visa at the consulate, then a residence permit in Bulgaria — usually for a year, renewable.

This is probably the question I hear most: "Venera, if I buy a flat in Bulgaria, will I get a residence permit?" My honest answer: an ordinary purchase — no. But there are quite a few grounds for moving to Bulgaria, and some are easier than they look. Bulgaria has been in the eurozone since 1 January, so I give all figures in euros too.

This is about citizens of non-EU countries and long-term residence — usually a one-year permit that can be renewed.

General requirements for everyone

Whatever route you choose, you will need:

  • housing in Bulgaria — a rental contract or your own home;
  • health insurance valid in Bulgaria;
  • sufficient means to live on — by law, no less than the minimum wage, pension or student grant;
  • a criminal record certificate for the first application.

Owning a flat helps a lot here: it covers the housing requirement. But on its own it is not a ground for residence.

Pensioners

This is one of the simplest routes. If you receive a pension — Bulgarian, from your home country or from another one — and you have sufficient means to live on, you can get a residence permit. No investment is needed.

The law does not name an exact amount. In practice it is safer to show income of at least the Bulgarian minimum wage: in 2026 that is €620 a month. More is even better.

Digital nomads

Since December 2025 Bulgaria has had a digital nomad visa. It is for people who work remotely for a company or clients outside Bulgaria:

  • income of at least 50 minimum monthly wages a year, which is about €31,000;
  • it suits employees of foreign companies, owners of businesses abroad and freelancers with foreign clients;
  • the permit is issued for one year and can be renewed once.

Property investment

Here is the "through the flat" route. You need to invest at least BGN 600,000 — about €306,775 — in Bulgarian real estate:

  • you can buy several properties, residential or commercial; the total counts;
  • the amount applies to each applicant, not to the family;
  • by the time you apply, everything must be paid through an account at a Bulgarian bank, and any outstanding loan may not exceed 25%;
  • you can also buy through a Bulgarian company if you own more than 50% of its capital.

The permit is issued for a year and renewed as long as the investment is kept. A spouse and minor children can get residence as family members.

Business, work, study, family

  • Your own company. You need to create at least 10 full-time jobs for Bulgarian citizens.
  • Regional investment. At least BGN 250,000 (about €127,800) in a less developed region, plus at least 5 jobs for Bulgarian citizens.
  • Trade representative office of a foreign company — for its representatives, if the company has the required turnover.
  • Work under a contract with a Bulgarian employer (single permit or EU Blue Card), study at a Bulgarian university, family — for example, marriage to a Bulgarian citizen.

How it works

  1. At the Bulgarian consulate in your country you get a national D visa.
  2. You come to Bulgaria and apply for residence at the Migration Directorate.
  3. You receive the card. The state fee for a one-year permit is BGN 500, about €256.

Applications are reviewed in 14 to 60 days. After 5 years of legal residence you can apply for permanent residence.

What this means for buyers

If you want both to buy a flat by the sea and to live in Bulgaria, I advise thinking about these two things separately. The purchase is your home and your investment. The residence route is best chosen to fit you: a pension, remote work, a business or a large investment.

One more thing: the rules are refined from time to time, and consulates may ask for extra documents. So before applying, always check the current list with the consulate or a lawyer.
